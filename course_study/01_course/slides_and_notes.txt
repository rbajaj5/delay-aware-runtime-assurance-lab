=== SLIDE 15 ===
What this study establishes
The finite heuristic monitor has a saturation region: when every candidate predicts violation, it repeatedly chooses the least-bad option. The region is a boundary shell at N=3-4, expands across N=5-9, and is nearly the whole envelope at N=10.
Two registered repairs avoid the region: the N=4 horizon repair and projection-style selection both reach 0/1000, but neither proves that the scorer itself is fixed.
The local lesson is narrower and useful: monitor sophistication is not safety by itself. These results are an implementation-specific counterexample, not a general limitation of delay-aware assurance.
Scope: one PPO checkpoint, one 2D plant, fixed initialization; off-diagonal cells are exploratory.
Next: formal safety filtering, a second checkpoint, and randomized initial states.
Artifacts: hash-manifested bundles for every block · prereg SHAs on file · figures reproducible from committed CSVs
[SPEAKER NOTES]
SAY: "Three conclusions. First, this finite heuristic monitor contains a saturation region whose size changes with the shared horizon. Second, E-N4 and projection-style selection avoid that region, but neither repairs the scorer itself. Third, monitor sophistication is not safety by itself. This is an implementation-specific counterexample, not a general limitation of delay-aware assurance. The next tests are formal filtering, additional checkpoints, randomized initial states, and plant mismatch." [About 34 seconds.]
