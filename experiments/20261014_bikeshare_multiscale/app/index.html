Evidence and limitations

    Mathematical introduction: historical Königsberg has four land regions and seven bridges, all four vertices of odd degree. There is no Eulerian tour. Bikeshare service does not require one: each trip has its own origin, destination, bicycle and available dock.

    The monthly rental-ranking chart is a design analogy only. This study contains seven observed days, not monthly observations. Maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Snapshot: October 2026. Demand: Capital Bikeshare, April 1–7, 2025.

    Topology is not resolution or fractal dimension. Adding or closing a connection changes route options. Network reduction changes model resolution, not a measured Hausdorff dimension. A circle and a Koch snowflake boundary have the same topological type but different Hausdorff dimensions. The Jordan curve theorem concerns planar separation; it does not show this transport network is fractal.
