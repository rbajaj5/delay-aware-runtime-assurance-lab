# Hex Delayed-Observation Simulation

A seeded 7x7 Hex board was filled in a random legal move order (seed 20260730). The delayed panel shows the true board from 3 moves earlier. A BFS crossing check verifies the final monochromatic connection: YELLOW top-bottom.

This movie is a pedagogical topology/delay analogy, not evidence about the v6 shield or a claim about strategic play under communication delay.
